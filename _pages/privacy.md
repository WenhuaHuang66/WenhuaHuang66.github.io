# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 2026**

## 1. Information We Collect

This website and the Compound Structure Search App are designed to minimize data collection.

### Information You Provide Voluntarily

- **Purchase information**: When you buy the App through Waffo Pancake, the payment processor collects your email address, name, and payment details. Seller receives only the email address and order confirmation — **we never see your credit card number** or full payment details.
- **Support emails**: When you contact us for support, we retain your email and correspondence to help resolve your issue.

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Website analytics**: This website may use basic analytics (e.g., Google Analytics or Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand visitor traffic patterns. This data is anonymous and does not identify individual users.
- **Local browser storage**: The Compound Structure Search App stores your recent search history **only in your browser's localStorage** on your device. This data never leaves your browser — it is **not uploaded to any server**.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

- ❌ We do **not** collect chemical compound names you search for
- ❌ We do **not** collect your location data
- ❌ We do **not** use cookies for tracking or advertising
- ❌ We do **not** share your information with third parties

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information we collect only to:
- Process your purchase and deliver the App
- Respond to your support inquiries
- Improve the website and App (anonymous analytics only)

## 4. Third-Party Services

### Payment Processing (Waffo Pancake)

Payments are processed by Waffo Pancake. Their privacy policy governs how your payment information is handled. Seller receives only:
- Your email address
- Order confirmation details (amount, date, order ID)

**We do NOT receive or store:**
- Your credit card number
- Your full billing address
- Your payment method details

### Chemical APIs (PubChem, OPSIN, MyMemory)

The App sends compound names to free public APIs to retrieve chemical structures. These APIs may log requests for their own purposes. No personally identifiable information (PII) is sent to these APIs.

### Cloudflare

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which provides DDoS protection and basic analytics. Cloudflare's privacy policy applies.

## 5. Data Retention

- **Purchase records**: Retained for 7 years (tax and legal requirements)
- **Support correspondence**: Retained for up to 2 years after resolution
- **Local search history**: Stored only on your device; you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser data

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
- Request access to personal data we hold about you
- Request correction or deletion of your data
- Opt out of analytics tracking

To exercise these rights, contact [csa-support@wenhuahuang.com](mailto:csa-support@wenhuahuang.com).

## 7. Children's Privacy

This website and App are not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
