Recently, I developed a **compound structure search tool** to solve a common pain point for teachers, students, and chemistry professionals: you know the name of a compound, and you urgently want to see its structure. But looking it up on large websites or using professional tools can be cumbersome — first, network speed may be slow; second, Chinese names are often not supported; third, search returns a flood of information that is overwhelming to sort through.

The tool I built supports searches using **both Chinese and English names**, and it runs very fast. While displaying the structural formula, it also shows the Chinese name, English name, and CAS number simultaneously — no other redundant information, so the interface is clean and clear. In addition, it keeps track of your **last five searches** for quick reference.

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
